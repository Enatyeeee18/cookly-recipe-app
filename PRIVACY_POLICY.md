# Cookly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

Cookly collects the email address, name, and password needed to create and authenticate an account. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are never stored in plain text.

Cookly stores saved recipe favorites for the signed-in account. Recipe data and account data are processed by the Cookly API and its production database provider.

Cookly does not sell personal information. Authentication tokens are stored locally on the device and are sent only to the Cookly API over HTTPS in production.

To request account deletion or ask privacy questions, contact the publisher at the support email listed on the Google Play store listing.

The service may update this policy when its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
